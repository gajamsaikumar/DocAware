RENTAL AGREEMENT

1. Parties
Landlord: ABC Properties
Tenant: Jane Smith

2. Deposit
A holding deposit of £500 is required. This deposit is non-refundable.

3. Renewal
This agreement will automatically renew for successive 12-month terms unless 60 days written notice is given.

4. Fees
An administrative fee of £50 applies to all changes to this agreement.

5. Termination
The landlord may terminate this agreement at any time without cause. The tenant must give 90 days written notice to terminate.

6. Liability
The landlord shall not be liable for any loss or damage to the tenant's personal property.
